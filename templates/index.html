Questions students ask

Can someone from outside Vaagdevi join?

No. This pass is exclusive to Vaagdevi students, and entry is checked against your college ID.

What do I need to bring?

Your e-pass, on your phone or printed, and your college ID. Both are checked at the gate.

Can I transfer my pass to a friend?

No. Each pass is linked to the student who registered.

How do I download my e-pass?

Once the organisers approve your payment, enter your reference ID in the “Already registered?” box above and click Get e-pass.
